# Video prompt vocabulary

Terms video models reliably understand, grouped by the part of the prompt they belong to. Use the plain-language phrasing on the left; models learned it from captions and film writing.

## Shot size

| Term | Frames |
| --- | --- |
| Extreme wide / establishing shot | The whole location; people are tiny |
| Wide shot | Full body with plenty of surroundings |
| Full shot | Head to toe |
| Medium shot | Waist up |
| Medium close-up | Chest up |
| Close-up | Face, or one object filling the frame |
| Extreme close-up / macro | An eye, a texture, a droplet |
| Insert | A detail cut into a scene (hands, a note, a dial) |
| Two-shot | Two people in frame |
| Over-the-shoulder | From behind one person toward another |
| POV | What a character sees |

## Camera angle

Eye level · low angle (subject looks powerful) · high angle (subject looks small) · bird's-eye / top-down · overhead flat-lay · Dutch angle (tilted, uneasy) · ground level.

## Camera movement

| Term | Movement |
| --- | --- |
| Static / locked-off | No movement |
| Push-in / dolly-in | Moves toward the subject |
| Pull-back / dolly-out | Moves away, often to reveal |
| Pan left/right | Rotates horizontally on the spot |
| Tilt up/down | Rotates vertically on the spot |
| Tracking / trucking shot | Moves alongside a moving subject |
| Orbit / arc | Circles the subject |
| Crane up/down | Rises or descends vertically |
| Drone / aerial | High, flowing movement over a landscape |
| Handheld | Organic shake, documentary or UGC feel |
| Gimbal / steadicam | Smooth walking movement |
| Whip pan | Very fast pan, often a transition |
| Rack focus | Focus shifts from one plane to another |
| Dolly zoom | Background stretches while the subject stays the same size |

Add speed: "very slow", "slow", "steady", "fast". Add the end point: "ending on a close-up of her hands".

## Lens and focus

- 14–24mm wide: expansive, dramatic perspective, slight distortion
- 35mm: natural, documentary, environmental
- 50mm: neutral, close to the eye
- 85–135mm: flattering portraits, compressed background
- Macro: tiny details
- Fisheye: skate, action, playful
- Anamorphic: wide cinematic look, oval bokeh, horizontal flares
- Shallow depth of field / bokeh vs deep focus

## Lighting

| Phrase | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Golden hour | Warm, low, long shadows |
| Blue hour / twilight | Cool, soft, glowing windows |
| Overcast / softbox | Even, flattering, low contrast |
| Hard midday sun | Harsh, high contrast |
| Backlit / rim light | Glowing edges, steam and hair light up |
| Side light / raking light | Reveals texture |
| Low-key | Mostly shadow, dramatic |
| High-key | Bright, few shadows, commercial |
| Practical lights | Lamps, candles, screens visible in the scene |
| Neon | Saturated colored light, night city |
| Volumetric light / god rays | Visible beams through haze or dust |
| Window light | Soft, directional, natural |

Name the source and direction: "warm lamp from the left", "cold monitor light on her face".

## Style and medium

Photorealistic · documentary · 35mm film / 16mm film · film grain · VHS / camcorder · black-and-white noir · commercial product photography · high-key beauty · 3D animation · stylized 3D / toy-like · claymation / stop-motion · cel-shaded 2D anime · hand-painted backgrounds · watercolor · ink wash · paper cut-out · isometric 3D · flat vector · whiteboard · glassmorphism UI · motion graphics.

Color grade: teal-and-amber · warm · cool · desaturated · pastel · high-contrast · muted naturalistic.

## Motion and physics words

Drifts, glides, ripples, billows, sways, flutters, splashes, pours, drips, sizzles, steams, shatters, bursts, unfolds, assembles, morphs. Slow motion, high-speed capture, time-lapse, seamless loop.

## Audio (models with native sound)

```
The barista says: "Oat or whole?"
Audio: espresso machine hiss, cups clinking, low café chatter, no music.
```

Keep one speaker per quoted line. Describe ambience and effects separately. Say "no music" or "silent" if you will add a soundtrack later.

## Worked example

Rough idea: *"cozy autumn ad for my candle shop, for Instagram"*

```
A hand lights an amber glass candle on a wooden windowsill; the flame catches and steadies as rain streaks down the window behind it. Slow push-in from a medium shot to a close-up of the flame, 50mm, shallow depth of field. Warm candlelight as the key, cool grey daylight from the window, soft bokeh of autumn leaves outside. Cozy, intimate lifestyle commercial look, no text. 4:5, 8 seconds.
```

- Settings: Kling or Veo, 4:5, 8 s. If the candle must match the real product, use image-to-video from a product photo and keep only the motion, camera and light lines.
- Negative: `text, logo, extra flames, melted label, flicker, blurry`.
