# Luma Ray prompting guide

**Model page:** [Luma Ray on Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/luma-ai)

Ray is Luma AI's video model family. It is known for natural, cinematic motion and for image-to-video that animates a still with believable camera movement and atmosphere.

## Where it shines

- Animating photos: listings, products, travel shots, portraits
- Atmospheric, cinematic b-roll (fog, light, weather)
- Natural camera moves that feel like a real rig
- Mood pieces and establishing shots

## Prompt tips

- **Write naturally.** Ray reads plain, descriptive language well; you don't need keyword lists.
- **In image-to-video, prompt the motion and the camera, not the scene.** "The camera slowly pushes in as mist drifts across the lake" is enough.
- **Add atmosphere for life.** Moving light, drifting haze, wind in foliage, rain — small environmental motion makes stills feel filmed.
- **Keep camera moves single and slow** for photos with straight lines (rooms, buildings).
- **Use start and end frames** when the tool offers them, to control where a move lands.

## Example prompts

```
Image-to-video: the camera slowly pushes in toward the cabin in the image while morning mist drifts across the lake in front of it and the pine trees sway gently. Keep the cabin and shoreline unchanged. Soft dawn light. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

```
A lone lighthouse on a rocky headland at dusk, its beam beginning to sweep as storm clouds roll in over a dark sea. Slow aerial drift toward the lighthouse. Deep blue and slate tones, the warm beam cutting through light rain. Cinematic, atmospheric. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

```
Close-up portrait of an elderly woman with silver braids sitting by a window, she slowly turns from the window toward the camera and her eyes crinkle into a smile. Very slow push-in, 85mm, shallow depth of field. Soft north-window light. Quiet, intimate documentary look. 4:5, 6 seconds.
```
