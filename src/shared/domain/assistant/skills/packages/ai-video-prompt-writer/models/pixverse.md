# PixVerse prompting guide

**Model page:** [PixVerse on Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/pixverse)

PixVerse is a video model popular with social creators for quick, eye-catching results: stylized looks, anime, playful effects and trend-style clips. It suits fast iteration where you want many variations of an idea.

## Where it shines

- Anime and stylized 3D characters
- Short, punchy social clips and trend formats
- Fun transformations and effects
- Quick drafts to explore an idea before a final render elsewhere

## Prompt tips

- **Name the style first and clearly**: "anime", "3D cartoon", "claymation", "cyberpunk", "realistic".
- **Keep prompts short and visual.** Subject, action, setting, one camera move. PixVerse responds well to compact prompts.
- **Exaggerate motion for social.** Big, readable actions read better on a phone than subtle ones.
- **Use image-to-video for character consistency** across a series of clips.
- **Generate variations** and pick the best rather than over-engineering one prompt.

## Example prompts

```
3D cartoon style: a chubby orange cat in a tiny astronaut suit floats inside a spaceship, chasing a floating fish-shaped snack, paws flailing. Static medium shot. Bright saturated colors, soft lighting, playful and funny. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

```
Anime style: a girl with long silver hair stands on a train platform as a speeding train passes, her hair and coat whipping in the wind, petals swirling around her. Low-angle shot, slight camera shake. Golden-hour light, vivid colors, dramatic mood. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

```
A plain white sneaker on a pedestal transforms into a glowing neon-cyberpunk version, circuit lines spreading across its surface, then sparks burst outward. Slow orbit. Dark background, magenta and cyan light. Striking social-ad style. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```
