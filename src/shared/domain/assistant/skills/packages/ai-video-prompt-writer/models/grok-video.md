# Grok Video prompting guide

**Model page:** [Grok Video on Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/grok-video)

Grok Video is xAI's video generation model. It is geared toward quick, playful generations from text or from an image, which makes it handy for social content and fast experimentation. Capabilities change between versions; check the model page for the current options (durations, aspect ratios, audio).

## Where it shines

- Fast idea exploration and social-first clips
- Animating a still image into a short moment
- Playful, imaginative scenes
- Variations of one concept

## Prompt tips

- **Keep it short and vivid.** One subject, one action, a setting and a style.
- **Describe the motion you want from a still** — a turn, a gesture, wind, light change — when animating an image.
- **State the style explicitly** (photoreal, anime, 3D cartoon) so results are consistent.
- **Iterate quickly**: generate several variants and refine the best prompt.
- **Avoid real people and brands**; describe original characters.

## Example prompts

```
A tiny hedgehog in a knitted beanie sips hot cocoa from a thimble on a snowy windowsill, then sneezes, puffing up its spines. Close-up, static camera. Warm interior glow, cool snowy light outside. Cozy photoreal style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

```
Image-to-video: the person in the image looks up from their book, notices snow starting to fall outside the window and smiles. Gentle push-in. Keep their appearance unchanged. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

```
A retro-futuristic city at sunset where flying cars glide between art-deco towers, a neon billboard flickers on. Slow drone pan across the skyline. Warm orange and teal palette, 1950s sci-fi illustration style. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```
