# Marey prompting guide

**Model page:** [Marey on Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/marey-realism)

Marey is Moonvalley's video model, built for professional and commercial production and trained on licensed footage. It is aimed at filmmakers who want realistic, controllable shots and a clear provenance for the training data behind them.

## Where it shines

- Filmic realism for commercials and branded content
- Deliberate camera direction and composition
- Productions where training-data provenance matters to the client
- Establishing shots, b-roll and inserts that cut with live action

## Prompt tips

- **Direct like a DP.** Specify shot size, lens, height, movement and the end frame of the move.
- **Describe the light physically**: source, direction, quality, color temperature ("soft north-window light, 5600K, gentle fill from a bounce").
- **Keep performance subtle.** Small, specific actions (a glance, a breath, a hand on a railing) read more real than big gestures.
- **Match your live-action footage**: state the grade, grain and aspect ratio you are cutting into.
- **Plan coverage**: wide, medium, close and insert of the same moment, with identical light and wardrobe lines.

## Example prompts

```
Wide establishing shot of a quiet harbor at blue hour, fishing boats moored in still water, a single figure walking along the quay with a lantern. Slow lateral dolly left to right, 40mm lens at eye height. Cool ambient light with warm lantern and window practicals. Natural film grain, restrained grade. 16:9, 8 seconds.
```

```
Insert shot: a man's weathered hands unwrap a cloth to reveal an old brass compass on a workbench, the needle settling. Top-down at 60 degrees, very slow push-in, 100mm macro. Single soft key from a high window, warm tungsten fill. Commercial realism. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

```
Medium close-up of a woman in her forties standing at a train window, the landscape passing in reflection over her face; she exhales and closes her eyes briefly. Static camera, 65mm, shallow depth of field. Overcast daylight, muted palette, subtle grain. 2.39:1 framing within 16:9, 6 seconds.
```
