# Vidu prompting guide

**Model page:** [Vidu on Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/vidu-ai)

Vidu is a video model from Shengshu Technology. It is often chosen for anime and stylized animation and for reference-based generation, where you supply images of a character or object and the model keeps them consistent in the clip.

## Where it shines

- Anime and 2D/3D stylized animation
- Keeping a character or product consistent from reference images
- Short character moments for series and social content
- Image-to-video for illustrations

## Prompt tips

- **Use references for consistency.** When the tool accepts reference images, supply the character (front-facing, clean background) and describe what they do.
- **Name the animation style precisely**: "cel-shaded 2D anime", "soft 3D animated film", "flat vector animation".
- **Describe one clear action** and the emotion behind it, shown through expression and pose.
- **Keep backgrounds described briefly** so the character stays the focus.
- **Repeat the character description** across prompts in a series even when using references.

## Example prompts

```
Cel-shaded 2D anime: a boy with messy black hair and a green scarf runs up a hill at sunset, stops at the top and raises his fist to the sky, grinning. Camera tracks behind him then swings to his face. Hand-painted clouds, warm orange and purple sky, soft bloom. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```

```
Using the reference image of the character: she sits at a café table, sips from a mug, notices something off screen and breaks into a surprised smile. Medium shot, static camera. Soft 3D animated-film style, warm afternoon light. 9:16, 5 seconds.
```

```
Flat vector animation: a delivery drone carries a small parcel over a stylized city of pastel buildings and lands it gently on a balcony, where a cat watches. Side-scrolling camera. Clean shapes, limited palette of mint, coral and cream. Friendly app-explainer style. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```
