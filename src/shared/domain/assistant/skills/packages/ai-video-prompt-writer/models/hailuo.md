# Hailuo prompting guide

**Model page:** [Hailuo on Vivideo](https://vivideo.ai/ai-models/hailuo-ai)

Hailuo is MiniMax's video model. It has a reputation for lively, expressive motion — characters that emote, dance and react — and for handling dynamic physical action such as splashes, falls and impacts.

## Where it shines

- Expressive characters and faces, including stylized ones
- Energetic movement: dance, sport, slapstick
- Liquids, particles and impacts
- Social clips where motion is the hook

## Prompt tips

- **Use strong, physical verbs.** "Leaps", "skids", "bursts", "shudders" give the model clear motion to animate.
- **Describe emotion through the body.** "She throws her head back laughing and slaps the table" rather than "she is very happy".
- **Give camera direction plainly.** Short instructions such as "camera tracks left", "camera pushes in", "static shot" work well.
- **Keep the scene simple.** One or two characters and a clear background keep the energy focused.
- **Image-to-video works well for character animation** — supply the character art and describe the performance.

## Example prompts

```
A golden retriever in a tiny chef's hat leaps onto a kitchen counter, skids across it on a dish towel and knocks a bag of flour into the air, freezing in a guilty pose as the flour settles over it. Static medium shot. Bright kitchen light, comedic timing, photoreal. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

```
A street dancer in an oversized orange hoodie performs a fast popping routine under a bridge, each hit sharp and precise, then freezes mid-pose. Camera tracks slowly in a half-circle. Graffiti walls, cool afternoon shade with a warm shaft of sunlight. Urban dance-video style. 9:16, 6 seconds.
```

```
A wave crashes over a black volcanic rock in slow motion, white spray exploding upward and droplets hanging in the air, backlit by a low sun. Static low-angle shot. Deep blue water, golden rim light, high detail. Nature commercial look. 16:9, 6 seconds.
```
